# Reports and takedown procedure

Internal. This is what the "Reports and removals" section of the Terms promises. Keep the two in step.

## Targets
| Case | First look | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Illegal, unsafe, or a threat to someone | within 24 hours | same day where possible |
| Fake offer, scam, copied photos, someone else's name or logo | within 3 working days | within 5 working days |
| Everything else (wrong details, spam, taste) | within 3 working days | within 7 working days |

Working days follow Bhutan. Out of hours, the target starts at the next working day.

## Steps (Admin → Moderation)
1. **Open the report.** Read the reason and details, and look at the promotion or banner as visitors see it.
2. **Decide:** Dismiss (no problem), Warn the business (fix it and keep it up), or Remove (take it down).
3. **If unsure or it may be illegal:** hide it first (Remove), then ask the legal adviser. It can be restored by
   contacting us; putting it back is easier than explaining why it stayed up.
4. **Tell the business** the reason in plain words when you warn or remove, and say how to reply or appeal.
5. **Tell the reporter** the outcome. Never name the reporter to the business.
6. **Record it.** The decision, who made it and when is saved with the report and in the audit log. Keep records for at
   least 12 months, longer if there is a dispute.

## Appeals
A business can write through the Contact page. A different admin than the one who decided looks again within 5 working
days, and replies with the result and the reason.

## Repeat or serious cases
Warn first, then limit, then suspend the business (Admin → Businesses → Suspend, which needs a written reason).
Coins already spent are not refunded when this happens (Terms, "Coins").

## Legal requests
A complaint from a lawyer, a court or a government body: do not decide alone. Pass it to the legal adviser the same
day, keep the original message, and do not change or remove records related to it.

## If you serve EU visitors later
The EU Digital Services Act asks for a clear way to report illegal content, a reason given for each removal, and a way
to appeal. This procedure already covers the substance; a lawyer should check the exact wording and any extra duties.
